THE IDEA / 01

Keep the feeder.
Change the future.

When a connected product loses its service, working hardware shouldn’t become waste. FeederNet is a community effort to give Petnet feeders a useful life beyond their original platform.

The project currently provides a self-hosted service that communicates with compatible feeders. It’s a hands-on project for people comfortable setting up software and configuring a network.
